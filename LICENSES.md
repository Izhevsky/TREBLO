# Licensing Registry

This repository may contain samples from different permitted sources. The license or permission basis for each external sample must be recorded in `SOURCES.md`.

## Preferred source order

1. Original user-created recordings
2. User-generated original material with documented applicable rights
3. CC0 material
4. Verified public-domain material
5. CC BY material with attribution preserved
6. Other permissive material only after its terms are checked

## Avoid by default

- material with unknown licensing
- files labelled merely "free" without usage terms
- non-commercial-only material when future commercial use is possible
- sample packs that permit musical use but prohibit redistribution of the raw WAV
- ripped audio from copyrighted commercial recordings without clearance

## Important distinction

Permission to use a sample in a finished musical work does not necessarily include permission to redistribute the raw sample in this public repository.

## Repository-level license

No blanket audio-sample license is asserted by this file. Each sample's provenance and applicable rights must be documented individually unless the repository owner later adopts an explicit license covering a defined subset of original material.
